GROUP TYPES — AISO Benchmark Query Framework
=============================================

A "group type" is defined by two things: what's in the query and how it's phrased.
Groups are generated by combining axes below. Every unique combination = a new group type.
Generate as many as possible, then trim by Google search volume + CPC (CPC = purchase intent proxy).


AXIS 1 — WHO IS NAMED
----------------------
- Nobody (unbranded)
- A competitor
- The client
- A person/role ("guide", "instructor", "expert")


AXIS 2 — GEOGRAPHIC LEVEL
--------------------------
- City
- Neighborhood
- Landmark
- Region


AXIS 3 — QUALIFIER
-------------------
- Demographic (families, couples, solo, groups)
- Price (affordable, luxury, cheap)
- Experience level (beginner, advanced)
- Occasion (date night, corporate, vacation)
- Vibe (adventurous, relaxing, educational, romantic)


AXIS 4 — PHRASING PATTERN
--------------------------
- "best X in Y"                              — recommendation request
- "where can I find X"                       — resource hunt
- "X vs Y"                                   — direct comparison
- "is X good for Y"                          — evaluation
- "how much does X cost"                     — transactional
- "I'm looking for X"                        — conversational intent
- "top rated X near Y"                       — social proof seeking
- "alternatives to X"                        — competitor displacement
- "what is the best X for someone who..."    — persona-based


EXAMPLE GROUP TYPES (axis combinations)
----------------------------------------
Group 1 — Unbranded Category Search
  Axis 1: Nobody | Axis 2: City | Axis 4: "best X in Y"
  Example: "best {service} in {city}"

Group 2 — Competitor-Anchored
  Axis 1: Competitor | Axis 2: City | Axis 4: "alternatives to X"
  Example: "alternatives to {competitor} in {city}"

Group 3 — Qualifier + Location
  Axis 1: Nobody | Axis 2: Landmark | Axis 3: Vibe | Axis 4: "best X in Y"
  Example: "best {service} near {landmark} for {vibe}"

Group 4 — Direct Brand
  Axis 1: Client | Axis 4: "is X good for Y"
  Example: "is {brand} good for {qualifier}"


FILTRATION
----------
- Generate all combinations
- Run through Google Keyword Planner or Ahrefs
- Sort by: search volume DESC, CPC DESC
- CPC is the purchase intent signal — advertisers pay for buying-intent queries
- Cut anything below volume/CPC threshold
- Remaining questions = final benchmark set
