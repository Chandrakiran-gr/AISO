ACTION PLAN — Next paying clients (no ideas, only steps)

Step 1. Get the list from Visit Santa Barbara
- Ask your father (chair): "Can I get a member list or directory of Visit Santa Barbara businesses — or at least the ones in tours, activities, outdoor, kayak, adventure?" If there's a PDF, Excel, or link, get it.

- Deliverable: list of 10+ (business name, website). Verify: you have at least 10 rows.

Step 2. Add one contact per name
- For each of the 10: find one way to reach them (email from site, contact form URL, or LinkedIn). Put in same list.
- Verify: 10 names each have 1 contact method.

Step 3. Send the pitch (same text each time)
- To each: one email or message. Use the Visit Santa Barbara angle: "I'm working with Visit Santa Barbara to help local operators show up where AI sends visitors. I run a report that shows exactly where to get listed and the links to do it. $400, one-time. Want a sample for your business?"
- Verify: 10 sent (track in a simple list: name, date sent).

Step 4. Follow up once
- 3–4 days later: for anyone who didn’t reply, send one short follow-up. "Following up — want me to run the report for you?"
- Verify: follow-up sent to every non-reply.

Step 5. Close or ask for referral
- If yes: send report, invoice, get paid. Mark done.
- If no: reply once: "No problem. Who’s one other operator you’d recommend I reach out to?" Add that name to your list and run from Step 2 for them.
- Verify: every reply has either (a) invoice sent or (b) one referral name added.

Run steps in order. Verify each step before moving to the next.
