THE IDEA (clear and simple)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

I have a product that works for any business: I run their name through my pipeline and get back a list of places where AI sends customers when people ask questions — plus the exact links to add their business there. I sell that as a report for $400.

Visitor bureaus (and chambers) in every city and county have member lists full of exactly these businesses: tours, activities, rentals, things that depend on being found. Same product, same pitch, new list every time.

So: get the member list from visitor bureaus everywhere. Contact those businesses. Sell the report. Automate the list-getting and outreach so I can do it for all counties. Revenue scales with how many lists I hit and how many say yes. Once I have proof, I can also sell to the bureaus themselves or to agencies — one deal = many businesses, faster path to $10K/month and up.


SPECIFIC STEPS TO ACTUALIZE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Phase 1 — Proof and first revenue (do first)
───────────────────────────────────────────
1. Get one list. Ask your father (chair, Visit Santa Barbara) for a member list or 10–15 names + websites of businesses in tours/activities/outdoor.
2. For each name: open their website, find contact email or contact page URL. Put in a list: name, website, contact.
3. Send one pitch to each. "I'm working with Visit Santa Barbara to help local operators show up where AI sends visitors. I run a report that shows exactly where to get listed and the links. $400, one-time. Want a sample?"
4. When someone says yes: run your pipeline for their business. Turn the output CSV into a simple report (list + 2–3 sentence intro). Send it. Invoice. Get paid.
5. Get 2–3 paid reports. Ask each for a one-sentence testimonial or permission to use their name. You now have proof.

Phase 2 — Manual scale (same process, more lists)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. Pick the next 3–5 regions. Google "[city or region] visitor bureau" or "[state] tourism member directory." Find the bureau site.
7. For each bureau: get the member list (download, request, or scrape their public directory). Filter for tours, activities, outdoor, rentals.
8. For each business on those lists: get contact (email or contact page). Add to your master list.
9. Send the same pitch (adjust "Visit Santa Barbara" to "[Bureau name]" or "visitor bureaus" if you're not tied to one). Track: sent, replied, closed.
10. Deliver reports, invoice, get paid. Follow up once on non-replies. Ask every "no" for one referral name.

Phase 3 — Automate acquisition (deploy for all counties)
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
11. Build or use a list of US counties (or metro/region names) and the visitor bureau URL or name for each. Source: Google "[county name] visitor bureau" or use a directory. Output: spreadsheet or DB of region → bureau name → bureau URL (and/or member list URL if public).
12. Automate "get member list per bureau": either (a) scrape public member directories, or (b) send a short template email to each bureau requesting member list / "businesses in tours and activities," or (c) both. Store results: bureau, business name, website, contact (when found).
13. Automate contact finding: for each business website, run a script or tool that finds contact email or contact page URL (e.g. crawl site for mailto: or /contact). Add to your prospect list.
14. Automate outreach: email sequence (pitch + one follow-up) to each prospect. Use a tool (e.g. mail merge, cold email platform) so you're not sending one by one. Track opens/replies in a simple sheet or CRM.
15. When someone replies yes: you (or a simple workflow) run the pipeline for that business, generate the report, send it, send invoice. Keep delivery simple until volume forces automation.

Phase 4 — Bureau and agency deals (faster path to $10K+/month)
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
16. With 2–3 testimonials and proof, pitch the first bureau: "I've run this for [names]. I can run it for every member in your region. We can do per-report or a monthly retainer. You offer it as a member benefit or I work with you to promote it." Close one.
17. Pitch 2–3 more bureaus in other regions. Same offer. Each deal = many reports or a recurring retainer.
18. Pitch SEO/marketing agencies that serve local/tourism clients: "I run AI visibility reports. I've done this for [X]. I can do it for your whole client list. $X per report or $Y/month retainer." Close 1–2. Two agency/bureau deals at $3–5K/month each = $10K/month.

Ongoing
──────
19. Every week: add new regions to the automated list, run outreach, close replies, deliver reports, collect payment.
20. Every month: review conversion (sent → reply → paid). Improve pitch or targeting if conversion is low. Raise price when demand is steady.
